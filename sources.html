Technical Credits

What isn't this project's own work

Everything in the AR/VR scenes is built from scratch for this project — procedural geometry, hand-painted canvas textures, an original logo — with one deliberate exception, credited here in full as its licence requires.

Human figure rig & walk animation

"Rigged Figure" — Khronos Group glTF-Sample-Assets, © 2017 Cesium, licensed CC BY 4.0. Used unmodified for geometry, skeleton and animation; its plain, untextured default material is recoloured per scene at runtime — no texture or trademark of any kind is used. 52KB.

Beyond Bengal

The Reading Room

This project sits inside a wider, ongoing conversation about digital storytelling and audio-visual heritage practice — none of it specific to Patachitra or Gajan, all of it useful for building this archive responsibly. Sources marked unconfirmed detail are real, locatable sources whose exact title or authorship this project has not been able to fully verify — flagged rather than guessed at.

Studies in Digital Heritage · 2019
Marsili, Giulia, and Lucia Maria Orlandi. "Digital Humanities and Cultural Heritage Preservation: The Case of the BYZART Project."

An integration case study (Byzantine Art on Europeana) for exactly the metadata-cataloguing repository type this project proposes.

Read ↗
IJHER · 2025
Shee, Sudipta. "Digital Preservation of Cultural Heritage in India: A Digital Age."

Surveys India's own infrastructure — National Digital Library, National Mission for Manuscripts, Digital India — the policy layer this project's community-archive model would eventually have to sit inside.

Read ↗
CAIS 2021 no stable link found
Shiri, Ali, Deanna Lyn Howard, and Sharon Farnel. "Indigenous Digital Storytelling for Cultural Heritage Access and Preservation."

University of Alberta research on Indigenous-led storytelling infrastructure — a close cousin to the "co-archivist" model proposed here. Located in the project's own reference folder; not linked because no stable public URL could be confirmed for it.

Eurographics GCH · 2018 no stable link found
Selmanović, E., et al. "VR Video Storytelling for Intangible Cultural Heritage Preservation."

Immersive-media approach to the same "how do you archive a performance" problem, from a computer-graphics angle rather than an ethnographic one.

e-IPH / ICIS 2022, Penang
"Audio-Visual Digital Preservation Strategies: A Case Study."

Conference paper from the 5th International Conference on Information Science, hosted by the Environment-Behaviour Proceedings journal, on practical AV preservation workflow.

Journal home ↗
IGI Global · 2015
"Digital Humanities in Cultural Preservation." Book chapter 9.

On libraries and cultural institutions adopting preservation policy — useful comparison for where a community archive sits relative to an institutional one.

Read ↗
Sensors (MDPI) · 2025
Chang, S., and J. Suh. "The Impact of Digital Storytelling on Presence, Immersion, Enjoyment, and Continued Usage Intention in VR-Based Museum Exhibitions."

Empirical data on whether immersive storytelling actually improves visitor engagement — relevant to any future VR/AR component of this archive.

Read ↗
Smart Learning Environments · 2014
Smeda, N., E. Dakich, and N. Sharda. "The Effectiveness of Digital Storytelling in the Classrooms: A Comprehensive Study."

Education-focused, but its findings on comprehension and engagement generalise to any storytelling-based heritage interface.

Read ↗
Sustainability (MDPI) · 2021
"Digital Storytelling in Cultural Heritage: Audience Engagement in the Interactive Documentary New Life."

Seven years of web-metrics on one interactive documentary — a model for evaluating whether a digital archive is actually being used, not just built.

Read ↗
arXiv preprint
Beguš, Nina. "World Wide Models: Literary Tools for Cultural AI."

On what literary/DH method can bring to how AI systems encounter culture — relevant background as this project considers AI-assisted cataloguing.

Read ↗
RSIS International
"Crafting Connection: Digital Storytelling in Community-Based Tourism."

Direct parallel to the tourism/commercialisation pressures on Patachitra and the Patachitra Mela.

Read ↗
Media Practice and Education · 2024
Scott, Daryl. "Videographic Criticism and the Art of Creative Thinking."

A framework for treating video-essay work itself as scholarship — relevant to how this project's own audio-visual recordings might be written about, not just archived.

Read ↗
Consensus (AI research tool)
Synthesized answer: "What impact does digital storytelling have on cultural preservation?"

Useful as a fast survey of the field's consensus — not a substitute for reading the primary sources above it draws on.

HiGHER-eu project
"Digital Storytelling: A Tool for Cultural Democratization and Local-Based Narration."

EU project framing digital storytelling explicitly as a democratization tool — echoes this project's "co-archivist, not informant" argument.
