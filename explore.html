Explore the Archive

Audio, video, interviews and interactive detail — choose a tradition, or browse the sources behind both.

This is where the archive itself lives — real field audio, interview transcripts, pigment recipes, sung scrolls, and an interactive, narrated walk through both traditions. Every recording below is from this project's own fieldwork; nothing here is a stock sample or a synthesized voice. Use the narrator button on any page to have it read aloud, and the sound toggle (bottom right) for an original ambient layer while you browse.

Gajan

Ritual sequence, five interview voices with real audio, a terms glossary, and the themes that came out of the fieldwork.

Patachitra

Natural-pigment recipe, three sung scrolls in Bengali and translation, the five roles inside a patua household, and an interactive scroll.

The digital-footprint map, living archive models this project learns from, and the full working bibliography.
Chitragāthā

A community-grounded digital-humanities archive of Gajan ritual and Patachitra scroll-painting traditions in Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal — built by two researchers from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, shared under Creative Commons with Indigenous-data-sovereignty safeguards.
