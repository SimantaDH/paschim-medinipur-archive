Explore — AR / VR Mode

Two original 3D scenes you can step inside: a Gajan ritual space and a Patachitra scroll-painting studio, built entirely from procedural geometry and hand-painted canvas textures — no photographs, no downloaded models, no borrowed art. Press Play a Film to choose a short, narrated cinematic film for a single voice, or the Full Documentary that moves through every voice in chapters — narrated in English or বাংলা (Bengali). Or skip straight to free exploration: on a WebXR-capable phone or headset, the AR and VR buttons below start a real immersive session through the browser's own navigator.xr API; everywhere else, drag to orbit the scene and tap a glowing marker for its story.

What's Actually Inside Each Scene

Gajan Ritual Space

A temple platform, a rotating charak wheel, a trishul, a dhak drum and a live particle-system flame — the film's seven chapters are drawn directly from the field interviews with Dipak Pramanik, Manjulata Pramanik, Soumen Chakrabarty, Ankan Karmakar and Nirmal Pramanik; the same elements stay explorable afterward as hotspots.

Patachitra Studio

An unrolling scroll carrying an original, procedurally painted narrative panel (generated fresh in your browser, in the pigment palette documented on the Patachitra page), orbited by floating pigment pots and a drifting brush — the film walks through the pigment recipe and the three named performers behind this project's three recorded songs: Shiuli, Ruksana and Sabera Chitrakar.

Chitragāthā

A community-grounded digital-humanities archive of Gajan ritual and Patachitra scroll-painting traditions in Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal — built by two researchers from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, shared under Creative Commons with Indigenous-data-sovereignty safeguards.
