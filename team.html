Our Team

Two researchers, one archive, built across two living traditions.

Simanta Nandi

MA, Digital Humanities & Social Sciences — IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

Simanta is an MA student in Digital Humanities & Social Sciences at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad. He completed the Summer Research Internship Programme 2026 at IIST Thiruvananthapuram, working on preserving cultural narratives, and holds a BA in Comparative Literature from Jadavpur University via CENTIL. He has taken part in the 15-Day UNESCO World Heritage Education Programme at Visva-Bharati and a British Library archival-digitisation workshop run with ILSR. His article, "Impact of Popular Culture and Media on Literary Reception in the Northeast of India," appears in Re-imagining Northeast Writing and Narratives: Language, Culture and Border Identities (Jamia Millia Islamia, 2024). His research interests span Digital Archiving, Cultural Heritage, AI for the Humanities, Electronic Literature and Digital Memory Studies. He was recognised by Oxford University Press India in 2021.

Disha Pramanik

MA, Digital Humanities & Social Sciences, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad · Course Coordinator, Centre for Folklore Studies and Research

Disha Pramanik is a literature student and researcher passionate about Digital Humanities and interdisciplinary research. She holds a Bachelor's degree in English Literature from Midnapore College (Autonomous) and a Diploma in Travel and Tourism Management from the same institution. Later, she completed her Master's degree in Digital Humanities and Social Sciences from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad. She has also completed a research internship at IIT Kharagpur, where she worked on stylometric analysis. She is also a blog writer at the Historica Foundation and currently works as a Course Coordinator at the Centre for Folklore Studies and Research. She is also a team member of DH Nexus, contributing to initiatives in the field of Digital Humanities. Her research interests include Digital Humanities, Literary Studies, Digital Archiving, Feminist Writing, Gender Studies, and Cultural Preservation.

Chitragāthā

A community-grounded digital-humanities archive of Gajan ritual and Patachitra scroll-painting traditions in Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal — built by two researchers from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, shared under Creative Commons with Indigenous-data-sovereignty safeguards.
